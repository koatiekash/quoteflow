Privacy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Who runs QuoteFlow. QuoteFlow is run by Christian Koat, an individual. Mailing address: 14203 SW 117th St, Miami, FL 33186, United States. The founder has chosen Florida, United States as the governing law for QuoteFlow.

What this note covers. This note covers two QuoteFlow websites:

  • the QuoteFlow waitlist at https://quoteflowfl.netlify.app
  • the QuoteFlow demo site at https://koatiekash.github.io/quoteflow

What we collect.

  • Waitlist: The waitlist form asks only for your email address and your trade (for example, plumber).
  • Checkout: If you choose "Get QuoteFlow" on the demo site, you're taken to a Stripe Checkout page for the $19/month QuoteFlow subscription. Stripe, not QuoteFlow, runs that checkout and collects payment and contact details under Stripe's terms.
  • Quote preview: The current quote tool builds the preview in your browser and does not send what you type to QuoteFlow.
  • Contact: If you use the demo site's contact link, it opens your own email app, and any message you send arrives at our email inbox.

How we use it. Information you submit on the waitlist is used only for QuoteFlow early-access updates. If you subscribe, what Stripe shares with us is used to provide your subscription. Messages you email us are used to reply to you.

We don't sell it. We do not sell your information.

Who handles it for us.

  • Netlify hosts the waitlist site and receives and stores waitlist form submissions.
  • Google (Gmail) receives email notifications of new waitlist signups, plus any messages you email us.
  • Stripe runs the $19/month checkout on the demo site and collects and processes payment and contact details under Stripe's terms.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) hosts the demo site.

How long we keep it. We keep waitlist signups until one month after the day the full QuoteFlow app goes live, then delete them. If you subscribe, we keep what Stripe shares with us while you're subscribed and for 30 days after you cancel. Stripe keeps its own payment records under Stripe's terms. Separate subscription terms are being prepared.

Changes. We may update this note. The current version and its effective date will be posted on the page.

Contact. For privacy questions or to be removed from the waitlist, email quoteflowfl@gmail.com. We'll handle removal requests through that address.
