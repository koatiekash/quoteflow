QuoteFlow subscription terms

Effective: November 10, 2026

Who you're dealing with. QuoteFlow is run by Christian Koat, an individual. Questions or cancellations: quoteflowfl@gmail.com. How we handle your information is in our privacy note.

What it costs. QuoteFlow costs $19 per month in US dollars. Stripe charges you when you subscribe and then once each billing period. Sales tax isn't currently added to the $19 price.

It renews until you cancel. Your subscription renews automatically every month until you cancel. There's no free trial. We may email you a reminder before a monthly charge. Either way, you can request cancellation at any time as described below.

What you're paying for right now. QuoteFlow is in early access, and the full app isn't live yet. Anyone can use the public QuoteFlow demo for free, with no subscription. It builds a quote preview in your browser and shows a preview of follow-up messages that are marked as not sent. It doesn't send real emails, run automatic follow-ups, or take deposits. Your $19 a month gets you the founding-member price below and the full app when it launches, at no extra cost. We'll email you when it's ready.

Launch day. "Launch day" means the date, in US Eastern Time, that we name as the full app's launch date in an email to subscribers sent at least 7 days in advance.

Founding-member price. If you subscribe before launch day, your QuoteFlow monthly plan stays at $19 per month for as long as your subscription continues without a break, with no time limit. If you cancel, or your subscription ends because of a failed payment, the founding-member price ends too. If you subscribe again, you pay the price at that time. Taxes aren't part of the locked price.

How to cancel. Email quoteflowfl@gmail.com from the email address you used at checkout and say you want to cancel. We'll cancel your subscription in Stripe and confirm by email, usually within 1 business day (Monday to Friday, excluding US federal holidays, Eastern Time). If Stripe bills you before we've processed your request, that charge may still go through.

When cancelling takes effect. Your subscription stays active until the end of the billing period you've already paid for. After that it stops, and you won't be charged again. Apart from the pre-launch refunds described under Refunds, we don't give partial refunds for the rest of a billing period.

Refunds. Before launch day, you can get a full refund of your payments by emailing quoteflowfl@gmail.com from the email address you used at checkout. Your request has to reach us before launch day. A refund cancels your subscription as soon as it's issued. Your access and your founding-member price end then, not at the end of the billing period. From launch day on, payments aren't refundable, except where the law requires a refund.

Price changes. If we change the price of a plan that doesn't have a locked price, we'll email you at least 30 days before the new price applies to you, so you can cancel first. A founding-member price doesn't change while it lasts.

If a payment fails. Stripe may try the charge again. If the payment still can't be collected, your subscription may end.

Changes to these terms. If we change these terms, we'll update this page. We'll email subscribers about any change that affects what they pay or what they get.

Governing law. These terms are governed by the laws of Florida, United States.
